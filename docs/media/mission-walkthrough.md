A silent walkthrough assembled from supplied application screenshots.
Each numbered item corresponds to one captioned video slide.

- **00–06 s · Explore Jezero crater** Start with the orbital map, departure time, battery and surface markers.
- **06–12 s · Describe the mission to Bellona** Two astronauts. Low battery. Ask for an energy-saving route and a sunlit charging stop.
- **12–18 s · Request a route** Bellona interprets the mission while the deterministic planner evaluates the terrain.
- **18–24 s · Inspect the route in 2D** Compare the planned path with the dashed shortest-path baseline.
- **24–30 s · Inspect the low-battery result in 3D** 12.2 km | 5.1 h | 20% battery on arrival | includes a sunlit charging stop.
- **30–36 s · Read the route briefing** Review distance, time, elevation, slope, battery and the charging-stop instructions.
